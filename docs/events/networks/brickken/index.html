PILLAR 03 Live Non-Chain Precedent

Sector 02 · Translating Live Precedents to Private RWAs

Adapting the CDR Architecture for Tokenized RWAs

PUBLIC EQUITIES LIVE NON-CHAIN

Traditional CDR (Cboe Canada)

  • Underlying: US Blue-Chip Equities (NVDA, AMZN).
  • Custodian: Canadian licensed trust (CIBC Mellon).
  • Clearing: CDS depository & exchange brokerages.
  • FX Management: Daily bank-managed currency adjustments.
PRIVATE RWAS

On-Chain Private Receipt (Brickken)

  • Underlying: Private Funds, Real Estate LPs, Mining Royalties.
  • Custodian/Trust: Licensed digital trust (Tetra) + GP Register.
  • Clearing: Permissioned smart contracts with compliance whitelist gates.
  • Settlement: Programmatic CAD stablecoin distribution & hold rules.

The Opportunity: Package private Canadian assets under the proven CDR model, tokenized via Brickken for 24/7 liquidity and instant cap table transparency.

Sector 02 · Settlement Architecture & Token Abstraction

White-Label Fiat & Token Abstraction

01

Familiar Investor Portal

Onboard via bank EFT, Interac, or CAD wire. Balances display in $CAD with zero visible Web3 wallet friction.

02

Escrow & Compliant Settlement
