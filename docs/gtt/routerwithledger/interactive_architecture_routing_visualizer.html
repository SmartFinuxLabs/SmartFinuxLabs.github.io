System Architecture & Event-Driven Pipeline

This section illustrates the decoupled architecture between upstream instruction creation, the dynamic Payment Orchestration Router, the double-entry Ledger Engine, and execution providers. By decoupling routing policy from execution rails, the engine evaluates real-time telemetry (latency, gas fees, liquidity) to select the optimal path before committing financial state changes.

Deterministic Scoring & Route Selection Engine

The router evaluates eligible execution capabilities by applying normalized scoring math clamped to $[0, 1]$. Adjust the policy weight sliders below to simulate how real-time strategy shifts (e.g., prioritizing low cost vs. high speed) re-rank executable rails in real-time.

Policy Weight Tuning (RP_GLOBAL_USDC_V3)

Cost Weight ($w_c$) 0.35

Lower cost increases normalized candidate score ($N_{cost}$).

Latency Weight ($w_l$) 0.25

Lower p95 latency increases normalized candidate score ($N_{lat}$).

Liquidity Weight ($w_q$) 0.25

Higher available balance increases normalized candidate score ($N_{liq}$).

Reliability Weight ($w_r$) 0.15

Lower 1h failure rate increases reliability score ($R$).

💡 Formula: $Final(c) = Base(c) \times P_{health}(c) \times P_{lat}(c)$ where Base is the weighted normalized sum.
