Private Sector · Example Pillar 02 · Regulatory Gatekeeper · NI 31-103 & NI 45-106

The Exempt Market Dealer (EMD) Framework

The Regulatory Triad

KYC & Identity: Validates investor identity, AML screening, and statutory exemption status (Accredited Investor or OM limits).

KYP & Due Diligence: Audits the fund business plan, financials, and legal LP agreements.

Suitability Assessment: Licensed Dealing Representatives certify risk matches client profiles.

ERC-3643 Tech Bridge

Trusted Claim Issuer: EMD compliance sign-off emits an on-chain attestation (addClaim) directly to the investor's ONCHAINID.

Protocol-Level Gating: Smart contracts block unapproved wallets and enforce NI 45-102 four-month seasoning holds automatically.

Connects legal suitability directly into EVM execution rules.

Turnkey Partnership

Division of Labor: Brickken delivers the software engine; the partner EMD provides the statutory dealer license and investor protection.

Speed to Market: Launch tokenized funds in 60–90 days without waiting 12+ months for custom licensing.

EMD handles bank escrow sign-off and provincial SEDAR+ Form 45-106F1 filings.

Operational Principle: Software cannot legally distribute securities in Canada. The EMD acts as the licensed off-chain oracle that legitimizes on-chain token transactions under provincial securities laws.

Public Infrastructure Sector · Example Pillar 03 · Settlement Rails

Payment & Stable Coin in Canada

Bridging Legacy Rails to Real-Time Settlement

Legacy Rail Constraints

Wire & Batch EFT Reliance: Unlike the US (instant ACH) or Europe (SEPA Instant), large Canadian institutional transfers still rely on manual wires and multi-day batch EFT clearing.

Escrow Friction: Capital calls and distributions face administrative friction through conventional Schedule I bank trust accounts.

Requires registered Canadian EMD escrow partners to bridge legal fiat custody.

Canadian Modernization

Open Banking Legislation: Canada's Consumer-Driven Banking framework unlocks bank-level API authentication, account validation, and automated subscription funding.

Real-Time Rail (RTR): Payments Canada's upcoming national infrastructure enables instant, irrevocable 24/7/365 settlement between participating institutions.

Creates the optimal domestic foundation for tokenized deposits and atomic fiat-to-token swaps.

Public Infrastructure Sector · Example Pillar 04 · Proven Market Precedent · BMO & Cboe Model

The Canadian Depositary Receipt (CDR) Blueprint

Fractional CAD Access

Global mega-cap shares traded directly in Canadian dollars on Cboe Canada, removing foreign exchange friction for domestic investors.

Eliminates high per-share price barriers and cross-border currency conversion fees.

Built-in Currency Hedge

Automated daily FX risk neutralization baked directly into the depositary receipt structure by CIBC and market makers.

Ensures investors capture pure asset performance independent of USD/CAD currency volatility.

Custodial Trust Model

Underlying shares are held 1:1 in institutional custody, while unitholders hold legally recognized fractional receipts approved by regulators.

A proven institutional precedent for packaging complex assets into domestic investment receipts.
