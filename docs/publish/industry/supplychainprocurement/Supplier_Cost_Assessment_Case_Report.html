01 / Executive assessment

Select Supplier 2 on annual cost

Supplier 2’s lower purchase price and freight rate outweigh its larger lot size and higher inventory requirements. Supplier 3 ranks second, followed by Supplier 1.

Recommendation: select Supplier 2 under the stated cost model. Its seven-week mean lead time and three-week lead-time standard deviation require 5,986 units of safety stock to support the modeled 95% cycle service level.

This is a cost-based award recommendation. Quality, supplier capacity, credit risk, resilience and contractual delivery commitments have not been scored because no evidence or weights were supplied.

07 / Model boundaries and sensitivity

Assumptions that make the results comparable

  • Annualized steady state: 52 operating weeks, stable mean demand, fixed order quantities and no special first-year inventory buildup.
  • Service target: 95% is interpreted as a cycle service level under a normal approximation. A 95% demand fill rate requires a different safety-stock calculation.
  • Variability: weekly demands are independent with the same mean and variance; replenishment lead time is independent of demand. All periods are measured consistently in weeks.
  • Inventory model: continuous review, inventory replenished in a batch, and average cycle inventory of Q/2. Safety stock uses the precise normal quantile and is rounded upward to whole units.
  • Base-case inventory value: purchase price only. Ownership begins at receipt; carrying cost on inventory in transit is excluded.
  • Freight: the stated rates apply uniformly, with the Supplier 2 minimum treated as a billable-weight floor. Taxes, duties, fuel surcharges, insurance, handling and other unquoted charges are excluded.
  • No added shortage cost: the service target drives safety stock. No stockout, lost-sales or expediting charge is provided or added.
  • Rounding: retain unrounded annual order frequency and intermediate calculations; round final currency to cents. Earlier approximate safety-stock calculations may differ by one or two units.

Sensitivity: include inbound freight in inventory value

If received inventory is valued at landed cost, replace c with c + F/Q in both inventory carrying components. Keep material and transport expenses separate as before.

08 / Buyer action

Turn the comparison into a sourcing decision

Confirm that the quoted lot sizes are fixed quantities rather than minimum orders or price-break thresholds. Verify freight terms, ownership transfer, capacity, quality and historical lead-time performance before awarding the contract. Under the confirmed base-case terms, use Supplier 2 as the cost benchmark and maintain the modeled safety-stock and reorder-point policy.
