Entity & Account Relationships in Rafiki Accounting

Rafiki operates on a strict separation between external customer-servicing ledgers (bank cores, digital wallets, fintech backends) and internal liquidity tracking ledgers (powered by TigerBeetle or PostgreSQL). Explore the relationship topology and operational walkthroughs below.

1. Ownership & Instance Boundary

Account Servicing Entities (Sender & Recipient): Each entity (wallet provider, bank, or fintech) hosts its own independent Rafiki deployment.

Core Ledger vs. Rafiki Ledger: Customer accounts (end-user balances) reside on the entity's core banking/wallet ledger. Rafiki never manages customer bank accounts directly; it only tracks internal liquidity to clear and forward Interledger (ILP) packets.

2. TigerBeetle Zero-Sum Balancing

Every deposit into a liquidity account is automatically offset by an equal credit to the Settlement Account. This ensures that across the Rafiki accounting instance, the sum of all debits strictly equals the sum of all credits at all times.

Σ Debits = Σ Credits ≡ 0

Account Categories & Invariable Rules

rafiki.dev/accounting
Account Type Main Purpose Scope / Count Balance Constraint
Asset Liquidity Supports FX / cross-currency packet forwarding. 1 per transacted asset ≥ 0 (Zero or Positive)
Peer Liquidity Tracks available credit line extended to an agreed peer. 1 per peer relationship ≥ 0 (Zero or Positive)
Outgoing Payment Liquidity Funds reserved to dispatch an Open Payments transaction. 1 per outgoing payment ≥ 0 (Zero or Positive)
Incoming Payment Liquidity Holds value received from completed Open Payments. 1 per incoming payment ≥ 0 (Zero or Positive)
Wallet Address Liquidity Holds value received via SPSP payments. 1 per wallet address (reused) ≥ 0 (Zero or Positive)
Settlement Account Tracks net funds deposited into Rafiki vs. withdrawn. 1 per transacted asset ≤ 0 (Zero or Negative)
Operational Lifecycle

3. Allowed Operations Matrix

Deposits (Inflow)
  • Asset Liquidity: Pre-funded to absorb cross-currency fluctuations and conversion requirements.
  • Peer Liquidity: Injected to establish or reset a peer's credit line after an external settlement.
  • Outgoing Payment Liquidity: Explicitly deposited from the core sender balance before dispatching an outgoing payment.
Withdrawals (Outflow)
  • Asset & Peer Liquidity: Withdrawn for rebalancing or reducing extended credit/capital.
  • Incoming Payment Liquidity: Withdrawn upon payment completion to credit the recipient core balance.
  • Wallet Address Liquidity: Withdrawn upon SPSP payment completion to credit the recipient.
  • Outgoing Payment Liquidity: Refunded if a payment fails or partially completes.
Payment Routing (ILP)
  • Inter-Instance Routing: Interledger packets route value across instances between Peer Liquidity Accounts according to established bilateral credit limits.
  • Intra-Instance Transfers: Multi-phase double-entry transfers move value between Payment Liquidity and Asset Liquidity Accounts when FX applies.
Walkthrough Case 01

Peer Liquidity Lifecycle: Cloud Nine Wallet Example

Full Resolution

Lifecycle Sequence Breakdown

Step 1
Credit Establishment

You extend a $100.00 USD line of credit to Cloud Nine Wallet, initializing their peer liquidity balance at $100.00 USD.

Step 2
Packet Processing

Incoming ILP packets draw down their peer liquidity balance toward $0.00 USD as payments clear.

Step 3
Limit Reached

Once the $100 ceiling is exhausted, Rafiki halts further incoming packet processing until external settlement occurs.

Step 4
Off-Ledger Settle

Cloud Nine Wallet transfers $100.00 USD directly to you over an agreed settlement rail (local clearing, wire).

Step 5
Liquidity Reset

Upon verifying receipt, you reset the peer liquidity account in Rafiki back to $100.00 USD, restoring routing capacity.

Walkthrough Case 02

Step-by-Step Liquidity Flow: Cross-Currency FX Example

Full Resolution
INITIAL STATE & TX #1 Successful Settlement
  • Initial State: Rafiki starts with two zero-scale accounts: EUR Liquidity = 10, USD Liquidity = 50.
  • Inflow: Rafiki receives €10 packets, moving 10 EUR from peer account into your EUR asset liquidity account (→ 20 EUR).
  • Conversion: FX rate converts €10 to $12 USD. Rafiki deducts 12 USD from USD Liquidity (→ 38 USD) while funding the payment account.
TX #2 ATTEMPT & FAILURE Automatic Reversal
  • Attempted Inflow: Transfer arrives with €50 packets, tentatively increasing EUR asset liquidity to 70 EUR ($20 + 50$).
  • Constraint Check: Applying FX yields a requirement of $55 USD against an available USD balance of only 38 USD. Because debits cannot exceed available liquidity, the transaction fails.
  • Reversal: The pending €50 transfer is rejected and reversed, restoring EUR asset liquidity back to 20 EUR.
